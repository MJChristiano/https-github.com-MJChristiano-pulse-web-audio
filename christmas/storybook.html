A Christmas story in three chapters

The Lantern in the Snow

On the quietest night of winter, a little lantern began looking for someone who needed its light.

Chapter One

The path appeared

Snow covered every road in the village. Then, beneath the moon, a trail of golden footprints appeared beside the old pine trees.

Chapter Two

A small light waited

At the end of the trail sat a lantern no taller than a teacup. Its flame did not burn hot. It glowed like a kind thought.

The lantern has a secret.

Chapter Three

Light is meant to travel

The lantern found its way from window to window, leaving every home a little warmer than before. By morning, the whole village was glowing.

A Christmas story in five chapters

The Lantern in the Snow

On the quietest night of winter, a little lantern began looking for someone who needed its light.

Chapter One

Nora saw a light

Snow covered every road in the village, and Nora could hardly see past the frosted window. Then a warm glow flickered beneath the old pine trees.

She pulled on her red boots and followed a trail of tiny golden footprints into the quiet night.

Chapter Two

A small light waited

At the end of the trail sat a lantern no taller than a teacup. Its flame did not burn hot. It glowed like a kind thought.

Nora lifted it carefully. The lantern brightened, and somewhere beyond the trees, a tiny bird called for help.

The lantern has a secret.

Chapter Three

A friend was waiting

Nora found a little robin shivering beneath a branch. Its nest had been covered by the snow, and it could not find the way home.

Holding the lantern high, Nora made a bright path through the trees. The robin followed her from branch to branch.

Chapter Four

The village shared its light

When Nora reached the village again, she told everyone about the robin. One neighbor brought a candle. Another brought a bell. A child brought a bright red ribbon.

Together, they made a trail of lights that led the robin safely back to its nest.

Chapter Five

Light is meant to travel

By morning, the snow had stopped. The robin was warm, the village was glowing, and Nora placed the little lantern in the window for anyone who might need its light.

Then Nora understood the lantern's secret: kindness grows brighter whenever it is shared.
